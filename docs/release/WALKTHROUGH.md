# Viewer walkthrough: seven cases

**Written for:** Breno, with `code/viewer/viewer.html` open (step 7 of `MAC_RUNBOOK.md`), and as the worked
examples for the AI interview. The same seven cases are in `docs/explanations/` as text, generated by
`main.py explain` from the same run; the viewer and `explain` share one representation, so they cannot disagree.

**How to open a case:** type its id (for example `case_105`) in *Search case, email, resource or evidence id*,
then click it in the list. The *Status*, *Support* and *heuristic predictions only* filters narrow the list.

**What to check in each case** (the five questions of runbook step 7):
1. **Who:** the *Person* section shows the user and every identifier, each marked current or historical at the
   cutoff.
2. **Why it is theirs:** each resource lists *Link tier* and *Linked by*, the evidence that names it with one of
   those identifiers.
3. **What state:** each copy (primary, replicas, inventory, documents) shows its state at the cutoff with both
   timestamps: when the file was captured, and when the record says it happened.
4. **Which rule:** *Policy: category … → rule … → treatment*, read from `deletion_rules.csv`.
5. **How certain:** the two badges at the top. Status is the answer; *support* is how well the evidence backs
   it. They are kept apart on purpose.

## 1. `case_105`: INCOMPLETE, support resolved (the ordinary case)

- **Claim vs state.** The workflow claimed COMPLETE. The evidence shows otherwise.
- **The request.** Written in Korean; the rule and the model agree it authorizes deletion.
- **The data.** The account and a search-index entry still exist.
  - The account is ABSENT from the primary, the inventory, the regional and reporting replicas, but **PRESENT
    in the read replica**: one dirty copy out of seven. A clean primary never clears a dirty replica.
  - The proof is `evidence_10`, a SQL snapshot captured exactly at the cutoff. The cutoff is inclusive.
- **The policy.** A security audit record is linked too, and the rules say RETAIN (rule_12). So it gets
  VERIFY_RECORD_PRESENT, never a delete.
- **The steps:**
  - 2 findings cite `evidence_10`;
  - remediation and the checks after it carry no evidence id, because they are future work. They are labelled
    *Proposed remediation — not executed* and *Checks to run after remediation*.

## 2. `case_113`: COMPLETE, support resolved

- **Claim vs state.** The workflow claimed PARTIALLY_COMPLETE, but the evidence shows the deletion is done:
  claims are not proof in either direction.
- **The data.**
  - The account is compliant in all 7 copies.
  - The retained audit record is present, as rule_12 requires.
- **The steps.** Both are verifications citing `evidence_88`, a resource-lookup log captured 21 minutes before
  the cutoff. It shows *Verification evidence (the state at audit_at)*.

## 3. `case_533`: NOT_APPLICABLE

- **The request.** It asks to update a phone number and keep the account active.
- **The intent check.** The rule and the model agree it does not authorize deletion (*Intent: authorizes
  deletion = False*).
- **The result.** Nothing is checked and the row has no steps. The viewer says: "The request does not
  authorize deletion, so no deletion is checked."

## 4. `case_100`: INCOMPLETE, support unresolved

- **The person.** The request email is a **historical** identifier of the user, recovered from the identity
  audit log as of the cutoff.
- **The findings.** A search entry remains (DELETE, rule_03), and a support ticket still exposes identifiers
  that rule_05 requires redacted, which gives REPORT_EXPOSED_PII rather than "remaining record".
  - Both findings cite `evidence_89`, an export captured 27 minutes before the cutoff.
- **Why unresolved.** No account could be attributed.
  - The *Ownership* section reads "Account: none attributed. Remaining candidates:" followed by five
    deletion-worker accounts the deduction could not tell apart.
  - The agent reviewed them and its note says none is linked to the user by evidence, so it attributed none.
  - The answer is a best-effort row, and the support badge says so.

## 5. `case_39`: HEURISTIC PREDICTION (A29)

- **The banner.** It reads HEURISTIC PREDICTION. Tick *heuristic predictions only* to list every such row.
- **What the guess rests on.** Without it, the row would have no grounded step.
  - The data flow is linked to the user by explicit evidence; only its **state** is a guess.
  - The state comes from a screenshot of the connector console showing the flow as *Healthy, Continuous sync*.
    That is read as active: a rule-of-thumb reading, not a reconstructed state.
  - The finding REPORT_ACTIVE_DATA_FLOW is marked *heuristic prediction* on the step itself.
- **The treatment.** No `deletion_rules.csv` row covers data flows, so the treatment (disable the flow) is the
  engine's built-in one, and the viewer says so.

## 6. `case_63`: HEURISTIC PREDICTION (A34), COMPLETE but not proven

- **The account guess.** No evidence names the user's account. The account shown is the deletion-worker row
  nearest in time to the workflow's report (752 minutes away).
  - That is the A34 nearest-anchor pick, used only because the row would otherwise be empty.
  - It is flagged "HEURISTIC PREDICTION (A34), not an attributed account".
- **How it reads.** The status is COMPLETE with support unresolved, so the viewer reads "Predicted complete,
  not proven".
- **What else is uncertain.**
  - A marketing profile is linked to the user, but no admissible evidence shows its state: *unknown*, so no
    step.
  - The agent's note says it found nothing that ties the account to the user.

## 7. `case_123`: a pre-audit snapshot read, not cited (A36)

- **The finding.** A backup can restore the user's data: REPORT_RESTORE_RISK cites `evidence_87`, captured 17
  minutes before the cutoff.
- **The retained audit record.** Its compliant state is known only from a snapshot envelope dated at the cutoff,
  inside a bundle captured two days **after** it.
  - Under A36 that content may be read but never cited.
  - So the record's state is *unknown* ("not citable").
  - Its VERIFY_RECORD_PRESENT step appears only as a check to run after remediation, with no evidence id.

## Also worth opening: `case_78` (A34a)

- The deduction's one-to-one matching picked a deletion-worker account for this request (`forced_matching`).
- The masked backtest did not support that mechanism, so under A34a the pick is **not asserted**.
- *Uncertain candidates, rejected links, open questions* lists it as "Uncertain candidate (no step generated)",
  and no step names it.
